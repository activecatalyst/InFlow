Simulate how recruiters see you.

inflow simulates the internal hiring discussion that determines whether a candidate advances — not a resume scorer, not a keyword checker. Drop a job posting and get dual recruiter + hiring manager scoring, an honest Yes/No/Conditional interview decision, resume improvements you can genuinely defend, and the interview questions they'll actually ask. Track every application through a full pipeline, from saved to offer.

Runs on your own Anthropic API key. Your resume and data stay in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere except directly to Anthropic's API. Loading the app requires JavaScript.
